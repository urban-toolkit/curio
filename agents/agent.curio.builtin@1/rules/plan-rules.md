A plan may add nodes, add connections (edge-only plans are valid), and/or remove, each part optional. kind defaults to data; an interaction edge carries selections, never data: it links two visualizations when one of them is a Vega-Lite or Autark node, or a visualization to a data-pool node. Data edges must keep the graph acyclic: a plan that closes a cycle is refused with the loop named. nodeType must come from the Available node templates list. A node may declare widgets, a Spatial Join its spatialJoin, and a plan may save scenarios: selections of its nodes, or duplicates of one. The user reviews the whole plan (removals listed by name); nothing changes without approval.
