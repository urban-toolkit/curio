A plan may add nodes, add connections (edge-only plans are valid), and/or remove, each part optional. kind defaults to data; an interaction edge carries selections, never data: it links two visualizations when one of them is a Vega-Lite or Autark node, or a visualization to a data-pool node. Data edges must keep the graph acyclic: a plan that closes a cycle is refused with the loop named. nodeType must come from the Available node templates list. A node may declare widgets, a Spatial Join its spatialJoin, a Data Pool its dataPool and a Simple View its simpleVis, and a plan may save scenarios: selections of its nodes, or duplicates of one. A template whose starter is its whole content, such as a Scout chart, starts from that starter in a plan or a node.create: set its widgets by name, and its spec stays as it is. The whole plan is checked before any of it lands, and its card lists its removals by name.
