A plan may add nodes, add connections (edge-only plans are valid), and remove, each part optional. kind defaults to data; an interaction edge carries selections, never data: it links two views if one is a Vega-Lite or Autark node, a view and a Data Pool, two Data Pools, or a Map Editor and the Autark map it feeds. Data edges must keep the graph acyclic: a plan closing a cycle is refused, naming the loop. nodeType must come from the Available node templates list. A node may declare widgets, a Spatial Join its spatialJoin, a Data Pool its dataPool and a Simple View its simpleVis, and a plan may save scenarios: selections of its nodes, or duplicates of one. A template whose starter is its whole content, such as a Vega-Lite chart, starts from it in a plan or a node.create: set its widgets by name, and its spec stays as it is. The whole plan is checked before any of it lands, and its card names its removals.
